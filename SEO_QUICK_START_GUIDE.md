## 🚀 DAY 1: GOOGLE SEARCH CONSOLE SETUP

### What to Do:
1. Go to https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Click "Start Now" and sign in with Google account
3. Add property: `mackenzielandscapes.uk`
4. Verify ownership using one of these methods:
   - **HTML file upload** (easiest)
   - DNS verification
   - Google Analytics
   - Google Tag Manager

### Submit Your Sitemap:
1. Once verified, go to "Sitemaps" in left menu
2. Enter: `sitemap.xml`
3. Click "Submit"
4. Wait 24-48 hours for Google to process

**Expected Result:** Google will start crawling and indexing your pages properly

## 📊 DAY 3: GOOGLE ANALYTICS 4 SETUP

### What to Do:
1. Go to https://analytics.google.com
2. Create account for "Mackenzie Landscapes"
3. Set up GA4 property
4. Get tracking code
5. Add tracking code to all pages (before `</head>` tag)

### Tracking Code Location:
Add this to every HTML page after line 37 (after meta tags):

```html
<!-- Google Analytics -->
<script async src="https://www.googletagmanager.com/gtag/js?id=G-XXXXXXXXXX"></script>
<script>
  window.dataLayer = window.dataLayer || [];
  function gtag(){dataLayer.push(arguments);}
  gtag('js', new Date());
  gtag('config', 'G-XXXXXXXXXX');
</script>
```

**Expected Result:** Track visitor behavior, traffic sources, and conversions

## ⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

1. ❌ Inconsistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) across platforms
2. ❌ Ignoring negative reviews
3. ❌ Not responding to customer questions on Google
4. ❌ Using stock photos instead of real project photos
5. ❌ Keyword stuffing in content
6. ❌ Buying fake reviews (Google will penalize you!)
7. ❌ Not updating Google Business Profile regularly
8. ❌ Forgetting to add location keywords

## 💡 PRO TIPS

1. **Post regularly on Google Business Profile** - Businesses that post weekly get 70% more actions
2. **Respond to reviews within 24 hours** - Shows you care and improves rankings
3. **Use high-quality photos** - Businesses with photos get 42% more direction requests
4. **Ask for reviews at the right time** - Right after project completion when customer is happiest
5. **Be specific with services** - "Patio Installation Kensington" ranks better than "Landscaping"
6. **Update business hours for holidays** - Prevents frustrated customers
