What We Offer

Our Services

Designs that bring outdoor spaces to life. From small courtyards to large family gardens, we shape layouts that balance form, function and atmosphere—always tailored to your lifestyle and property.

  • Bespoke layout, lighting and planting design
  • 3D visualisations and detailed planting schedules
  • Coordination with architects or interior designers

Built with precision and lasting quality. Our in-house teams handle every construction stage, from groundworks and paving to bespoke joinery and water features—always with tidy sites and considerate neighbours.

  • Natural stone, clay and porcelain paving
  • Bespoke timber and steel features
  • Fencing, decking, driveways and retaining walls
  • Dedicated project lead with regular progress updates

Ongoing care that keeps your garden thriving. The same team that understands the original design nurtures planting, soil and irrigation so the space matures beautifully season after season.

  • Tailored maintenance schedules
  • Soil health and irrigation checks
  • Seasonal planting and pruning plans
  • Optional quarterly garden reviews
