### 7. REVIEWS STRATEGY

**Goal:** Achieve 50+ reviews with 4.8+ star average

**Action Plan:**
1. Ask satisfied customers for reviews immediately after project completion
2. Send follow-up email with direct review link
3. Respond to ALL reviews within 24-48 hours
4. Thank positive reviewers and address concerns professionally
5. Never incentivize reviews (against Google policy)

**Review Request Template:**
"Hi [Name], we're thrilled you're happy with your new garden! Would you mind sharing your experience on Google? It helps other homeowners discover our work. Here's the link: [Google Review Link]. Thank you!"
