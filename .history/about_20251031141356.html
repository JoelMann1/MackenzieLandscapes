About us

Meet Mackenzie Landscapes

At Mackenzie Landscapes we believe your outdoor space is an extension of your home—a place to relax, entertain and connect with nature. Every garden we shape is designed to feel calm, functional and deeply personal.

Founded by a new generation of landscaping professionals, our vision blends refined design with practical craftsmanship so the spaces we deliver feel timeless and live beautifully.

Our Story

Mackenzie Landscapes was founded on a deep connection to the outdoors, shaped by a New Zealand upbringing surrounded by natural beauty and hands-on garden-making. From early on, our founder understood that exceptional landscape design is about more than plants and paving—it's about atmosphere, purpose, and flow.

After working with high-end landscaping teams across New Zealand, the UK, and further abroad, and studying landscape architecture, we brought that global experience and design-led approach to London. Today, we serve Central, South West, and Greater London with a commitment to meticulous craftsmanship, creativity, and care.

Our approach to landscape design is guided by the relationship between structure and softness—the clean lines of built forms paired with the natural flow of planting. Inspired by the balance between order and organic movement, where defined geometry meets layered, seasonal borders.

Planting is never an afterthought; it's a key design tool. Through thoughtful combinations, we use plants to lift and transform a space—adding depth, texture, and rhythm that evolves over time.

At Mackenzie Landscapes, every garden is a response to its surroundings. The goal is always the same: to create spaces that feel effortless, grounded, and alive—where architecture and nature coexist in harmony.

What We Stand For
