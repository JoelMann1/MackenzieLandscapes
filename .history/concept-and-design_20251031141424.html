What’s included

Concept plans

Scaled layout drawings that map zones, circulation, and key features so every element has a purpose.

Planting design

Layered planting palettes with seasonal interest, colour stories, and maintenance notes tailored to your microclimate.

3D visualisations

Optional 3D renders and mood boards to help you visualise materials, lighting, and the overall atmosphere.

Our design process

  • 01

    Discovery workshop

    We gather inspiration, measure the site, and clarify how you want to use the space day-to-day.

  • 02

    Concept development

    Layouts, mood boards, and planting directions are refined in collaboration with you until they feel just right.

  • 03

    Technical detailing / planning support

    We produce drawings and specifications ready for planning approvals and pricing by our build team.
