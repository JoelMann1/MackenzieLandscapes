Investment guide

Pricing & Consultation

Transparent, tailored proposals for gardens that reflect your vision. From compact courtyards to full estate redesigns, we price honestly and fix quotes once the scope is agreed.

How it works

Three phases. One clear journey.

From initial site assessment through to final handover, our process is designed for clarity and collaboration. You’ll know exactly what to expect at every stage.

Phase 01

Site Visit & Discovery

We measure the space, assess soil conditions and sun exposure, and discuss your vision. This initial meeting helps us understand constraints, opportunities and what success looks like for you.

Complimentary for projects over £15,000

Phase 02

Design & Planning

We develop concept drawings, 3D visualisations and planting schedules tailored to your brief. Through collaborative review sessions, we refine materials, lighting and layout until everything feels right.

Design fees from £2,500 (credited against build)

Phase 03

Build & Execution

Once the design is approved, you receive a detailed quote with no hidden costs. Our teams manage every detail—from materials procurement to final planting—with weekly updates throughout.

Most projects complete within 2-8 weeks

Investment factors

What influences project cost

Every garden is priced individually based on site conditions, material choices and design complexity. Here are the key factors we consider when preparing your proposal.

Site Access & Preparation

Narrow side passages, restricted vehicle access or significant level changes require additional planning and equipment.

Materials & Finishes

Natural stone, bespoke joinery and premium planting stock command higher investment than standard alternatives.

Design Complexity

Water features, integrated lighting systems and multi-level terracing add layers of technical coordination.

Project Timeline

Seasonal planting windows, planning permissions and coordination with other trades can influence scheduling and costs.
