Tree services

Pruning & shaping

Formative pruning, crown thinning, and reductions that respect the natural structure of each species.

Tree health care

Pest and disease diagnostics, soil aeration, and nutrition plans to improve resilience and longevity.

Removals & stumps

Safe dismantling, felling, and stump grinding when trees outgrow their space or pose a risk.

Safety-first methodology

  • 01

    Assessment & permissions (TPO checks)

    We inspect each tree, check for preservation orders, and submit any required council notifications.

  • 02

    Arborist team on site

    Climbers and grounds crew execute the work using low-impact rigging and eco-friendly equipment.

  • 03

    Aftercare guidance

    You receive pruning schedules, watering advice, and recommendations for future monitoring.

Emergency call-outs

Storm damage or hazardous limbs? We operate a rapid response service for urgent tree works across London.
