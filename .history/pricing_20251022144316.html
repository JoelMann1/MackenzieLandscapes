Investment guide

Pricing & Consultation

Every garden is unique. We create bespoke proposals tailored to your space, lifestyle and vision—with transparent pricing and fixed quotes once the scope is agreed.

Our approach

Consultation-based pricing

We don’t believe in one-size-fits-all packages. Instead, we work closely with you to understand your site, aspirations and budget—then craft a proposal that reflects the true scope and quality of your project.

From compact courtyard transformations to full estate redesigns, every commission begins with a conversation and a site visit.

How it works

Your journey from consultation to completion

Our three-phase process ensures clarity, collaboration and confidence at every stage—from initial site assessment through to final planting and handover.

Phase 01

Site Visit & Discovery

We begin with a comprehensive site assessment, measuring the space, understanding constraints and discussing your vision. This includes soil analysis, sun mapping and neighbour considerations.

Complimentary initial consultation for projects over £15,000

Phase 02

Design & Planning

Our studio develops concept drawings, 3D visualisations and detailed planting schedules. We refine materials, lighting and layout through collaborative review sessions until the design feels right.

Design fees from £2,500, credited against build costs

Phase 03

Build & Execution

With design approved, we provide a fixed-price quote covering all materials, labour and project management. Our in-house teams handle construction with weekly progress reports and quality oversight.

Typical builds: 2-8 weeks depending on scope

Investment factors

What influences project cost

Every garden is priced individually based on site conditions, material choices and design complexity. Here are the key factors we consider when preparing your proposal.

Site Access & Preparation

Narrow side passages, restricted vehicle access or significant level changes require additional planning and equipment.

Materials & Finishes

Natural stone, bespoke joinery and premium planting stock command higher investment than standard alternatives.

Design Complexity

Water features, integrated lighting systems and multi-level terracing add layers of technical coordination.

Project Timeline

Seasonal planting windows, planning permissions and coordination with other trades can influence scheduling and costs.
