Studio approach

A considered, collaborative programme

Our leads work alongside architects, interior designers and estate managers to deliver projects with minimal disruption. Each stage is documented and signed off before the next begins.

  1. Phase 01

    Discovery & mapping

    Site analysis, measured surveys and mood exploration sessions build the creative direction.

    Includes arboricultural liaison, planning insight and neighbour communications.

  2. Phase 02

    Detail & procurement

    Drawings, planting schedules, lighting plans and bespoke fabrications prepared for sign-off.

    We secure materials early, coordinating with artisans to protect lead times.

  3. Phase 03

    Build oversight

    Dedicated site management with weekly reports, quality control and client walkthroughs.

    Our team supervises every pour, fix and planting day to preserve the design intent.

  4. Phase 04

    Stewardship

    Post-completion tuning, horticultural aftercare and seasonal enhancements led by the original design team.

    Includes ongoing soil health checks, irrigation calibration and plant sourcing.
