How pricing works

We keep costs transparent with phased proposals. Most projects move through the following steps:

  • 01

    Site visit

    We survey the space, discuss goals, and provide a detailed brief with estimated ranges.

  • 02

    Design & planning

    Concepts, planting plans, and material specifications are refined with you before we price the build.

  • 03

    Fixed quote

    Once drawings are signed off, we submit a fixed quotation outlining scope, timeline, and payment stages.
