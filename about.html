Our story

About Mackenzie Landscapes

We are a small, detail-obsessed team focused on simple, durable landscapes that are built to last and easy to live with.

Our approach

Every garden begins with understanding how you want to live outdoors. From there we design spaces that balance structure, planting, and long-term care.

  • Design first: we prototype layouts and planting before we build.
  • Materials that age well: stone, hardwoods, and low-water planting.
  • Transparent pricing, tidy sites, and clear communication.

Facts & figures

12 specialists across design, build, and horticulture.

140+ projects delivered around Greater London.

Values that guide our work

Design integrity

We pair structural clarity with planting character so every space feels intentional year-round.

Considered materials

Sustainably sourced timbers, locally quarried stone, and smart irrigation keep your garden resilient.

Care that lasts

We stay involved after installation with maintenance plans tailored to how you use your space.
